About

Study resources, easier to find.

QMarketplace is a catalog and checkout experience for academic study materials. Students can explore materials by title, course, and university, review listing details, and purchase available resources.

How the marketplace works

Listings describe resources supplied by their contributors. A listing's presence does not mean that QMarketplace or a university has reviewed, verified, or endorsed its academic accuracy.

Built around clear information

Each listing should make its price and available details visible before checkout. Payments are confirmed on the server before an order is recorded as paid. Private downloads are available only when the purchase and file storage are configured.
