Copyright and content concerns

Draft notice workflow. A formal notice-and-takedown process and a designated contact have not yet been configured for this deployment.

Only upload or distribute materials you are authorised to share. Do not submit material that infringes another person's copyright, violates academic or institutional rules, or contains personal information without permission.

Reporting a concern

Contact the marketplace operator using a published support channel and include the listing URL, the material at issue, your relationship to the rights holder, and the action requested. Do not include unnecessary sensitive personal information.

The operator must establish a monitored notice channel and a reviewed removal and counter-notice process before accepting public uploads.
