Refund information

Not a final refund policy. The marketplace operator must determine the applicable refund terms, publish a monitored support contact, and obtain legal review before taking live payments.

This application does not currently provide an automated refund request or refund-processing feature. Contact the marketplace operator using the support details for the specific deployment if you have a payment or access issue.

A completed payment is recorded only after server-side PayPal confirmation. If PayPal shows a charge but your QMarketplace order is not confirmed, retain your PayPal transaction reference and contact the operator.

Do not treat this draft as a promise of eligibility or timing. Any published customer refund terms must be consistent with PayPal requirements and applicable consumer-protection laws.
